## 1. Introduction to Trigonometry
Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies relationships between side lengths and angles of triangles. In Class 10, trigonometry focuses primarily on **right-angled triangles**.

> **Right-Angled Triangle Setup:** Consider a right-angled triangle $\Delta ABC$, right-angled at $B$. For a chosen acute angle $\theta = \angle C$:
> - **Hypotenuse ($H$):** Side opposite to the right angle ($90^\circ$).
> - **Opposite / Perpendicular ($P$):** Side opposite to the angle $\theta$.
> - **Adjacent / Base ($B$):** Side adjacent to the angle $\theta$.

---

## 2. Basic Trigonometric Ratios (T-Ratios)

The six basic trigonometric ratios for an acute angle $\theta$ are defined as:

| Ratio Name | Abbreviation | Formula (Sides) | Reciprocal Relation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sine** | $\sin\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{P}{H}$ | $\sin\theta = \frac{1}{\csc\theta}$ |
| **Cosine** | $\cos\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Base}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{B}{H}$ | $\cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sec\theta}$ |
| **Tangent** | $\tan\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Base}} = \frac{P}{B}$ | $\tan\theta = \frac{1}{\cot\theta}$ |
| **Cosecant** | $\csc\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Hypotenuse}}{\text{Perpendicular}} = \frac{H}{P}$ | $\csc\theta = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}$ |
| **Secant** | $\sec\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Hypotenuse}}{\text{Base}} = \frac{H}{B}$ | $\sec\theta = \frac{1}{\cos\theta}$ |
| **Cotangent** | $\cot\theta$ | $\frac{\text{Base}}{\text{Perpendicular}} = \frac{B}{P}$ | $\cot\theta = \frac{1}{\tan\theta}$ |

---

## 3. Quotient Relations

$$\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$$

$$\cot\theta = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$$

## 5. Fundamental Trigonometric Identities

For any acute angle $\theta$:

1. **Pythagorean Identity 1:**
$$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$
$$\implies \sin^2\theta = 1 - \cos^2\theta \quad \text{and} \quad \cos^2\theta = 1 - \sin^2\theta$$

2. **Pythagorean Identity 2:**
$$1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta$$
$$\implies \sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta - 1$$

3. **Pythagorean Identity 3:**
$$1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta$$
$$\implies \csc^2\theta - \cot^2\theta = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta - 1$$

---

## 6. Key Takeaways & Summary

- **Reference Angle Matters:** Perpendicular ($P$) and Base ($B$) swap positions depending on which acute angle ($\angle A$ or $\angle C$) is being evaluated. Hypotenuse ($H$) always remains fixed.
- **Identity Applications:** Expressing all trigonometric functions in terms of $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$ is often the fastest way to prove complex trigonometric identities.
- **Value Bounds:** For acute angles, $0 \le \sin\theta \le 1$ and $0 \le \cos\theta \le 1$.
