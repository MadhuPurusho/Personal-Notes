## Meaning of Development

Development refers to the improvement in the quality of life and well-being of people.

Different people can have different ideas about development because their needs and goals may differ.

## Different Developmental Goals

| Person | Developmental Goal |
|---|---|
| Landless rural worker | More days of work and better wages |
| Farmer | Higher income and better irrigation |
| Student | Quality education and better career opportunities |
| Woman | Equal opportunities and freedom |
| Worker | Better salary, job security and safe working conditions |

## Important Points

- Development does not mean only an increase in income.
- People look for things such as:
  - Better education
  - Good health
  - Equal treatment
  - Freedom
  - Security
  - Job opportunities
  - Clean environment
- Different people may have conflicting developmental goals.
- A development goal that benefits one person may sometimes harm another person.

## Example

A factory may provide jobs and increase income for local people. However, if it causes pollution, it may negatively affect the health and environment of nearby residents.

Therefore, development involves both economic and non-economic factors.
