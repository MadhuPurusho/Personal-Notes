## Formal Sector of Credit

The formal sector includes credit provided by financial institutions that are supervised and regulated by the government and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

### Sources

- Commercial banks
- Cooperative societies

### Features

- Regulated by the RBI and government authorities.
- Interest rates are generally regulated.
- Proper documents and procedures are required.
- Borrowers usually need to provide information about their income and ability to repay.
- Banks and cooperatives provide loans for agriculture, businesses, education, housing and other purposes.

---

## Informal Sector of Credit

The informal sector includes lenders who are not supervised by financial authorities in the same way as formal financial institutions.

### Sources

- Moneylenders
- Traders
- Employers
- Relatives and friends

### Features

- Not regulated like banks and cooperatives.
- Interest rates can be high.
- Documentation may be limited.
- Loan conditions may vary between lenders.
- Borrowers may face difficulties if they cannot repay the loan.

---

## Formal vs Informal Credit

| Formal Sector | Informal Sector |
|---|---|
| Banks and cooperative societies | Moneylenders, traders, employers, relatives and friends |
| Regulated by authorities | Not regulated in the same way |
| Interest rates are regulated | Interest rates may be high |
| Proper documentation is generally required | Documentation may be limited |
| Follows established rules | Terms can vary between lenders |

## Importance of Formal Credit

- Provides credit through regulated institutions.
- Helps reduce dependence on informal lenders.
- Makes loans available for productive activities.
- Supports agriculture, businesses and households.

## Key Point

The formal sector is regulated and follows established rules, while the informal sector is generally outside the formal regulatory system.

# Quick Revision

Formal → Banks + Cooperative societies → Regulated

Informal → Moneylenders + Traders + Employers + Relatives/Friends → Less regulated
