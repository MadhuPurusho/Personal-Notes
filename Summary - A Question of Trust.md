## Chapter 4: A Question of Trust
*By Victor Canning*

> **Core Concept:** "A Question of Trust" revolves around the classic proverb **"Honesty among thieves."** The story highlights how overconfidence, blind trust, and falling for appearances can lead even the most meticulous planner into a trap.

## Comprehensive Summary & Plot Breakdown

### 1. Horace Danby's Secret Passion & Method
1. **Public Persona:** Horace runs a successful lock-making business with two assistants. He is generally healthy, except for seasonal attacks of **hay fever** in summer.
2. **The Secret Hobby:** Horace loves **rare and expensive books**. To afford them through an agent, he robs **one safe every year**.
3. **Meticulous Strategy:** Horace plans every heist weeks in advance. He only targets rich people and steals enough to last twelve months.

### 2. The Intrusion at Shotover Grange
1. **The Target:** Horace studies **Shotover Grange** for two weeks, targeting its safe containing **£15,000 worth of jewels**.
2. **The Opportunity:** He waits until the two servants leave for the cinema.
3. **Execution & Weakness:** 
   - Horace enters through the garden.
   - He makes friends with the pet dog, **Sherry**, by calling it by name.
   - The scent of flowers on the table triggers his **hay fever**, causing him to sneeze repeatedly.

### 3. The Encounter with the Lady in Red
As Horace prepares his tools to open the safe, he is caught by a calm, voice-of-authority young woman dressed in red.

Horace Sneeze Triggered -> Interrupted by Young Lady -> Lady Claims Ownership -> Deceptive Bargain Made

- **The Illusion:** She carries herself as the mistress of the house returning unexpectedly.
- **The Bargain:** She promises not to report Horace to the police if he opens the safe for her, claiming she forgot the combination code and needs the jewels for a party.
- **The Critical Blunder:** Anxious to avoid arrest, Horace takes off his **gloves** to light her cigarette and proceeds to crack the safe without wearing them.

### 4. The Arrest and Aftermath
1. **The Trap Closes:** Horace hands her the jewels and leaves, relieved that he escaped.
2. **The Investigation:** Two days later, a policeman arrests Horace for the burglary. His **fingerprints** were left all over the room, safe, and lighter.
3. **The Final Twist:** The real mistress of Shotover Grange turns out to be a **60-year-old woman with gray hair**. The woman in red was actually a rival thief who used Horace to do the hard work for her.

## Key Themes, Motifs & Symbolism

### 1. "Honesty Among Thieves"
- **Paradox:** Horace believed in an unwritten moral code among criminals. He trusted the woman's word because he believed a fellow member of the upper class or criminal world wouldn't betray him.
- **Outcome:** The story proves that **there is no honor or honesty among thieves**.

### 2. Deception through Appearances
- Horace appears to be an honorable citizen, but is a burglar.
- The young woman appears to be an aristocratic homeowner, but is a cold-blooded criminal.

### 3. The Symbolism of Hay Fever
- Horace's allergy acts as his physical **Achilles' heel**. It leads to the sneezes that reveal his presence and causes the confusion that leads him to take off his gloves.

## Important Practice Questions & Model Answers

### Q1: Was Horace Danby a typical thief? Explain.
> **Answer:** No, Horace Danby was not a typical thief. He was a 50-year-old well-to-do bachelor who ran a successful lock-making business. He did not steal out of poverty, greed, or violent motives. He robbed only one rich person a year to fund his passion for collecting rare and expensive books, and he never intended to harm anyone.

### Q2: How did the young lady manage to trick Horace Danby so easily?
1. **Composure & Authority:** She showed zero panic, acting completely natural and speaking with firm authority.
2. **Psychological Manipulation:** She played on his fear of going to prison, making him eager to please her.
3. **Distraction:** She got him to light her cigarette, forcing him to remove his gloves and leave his fingerprints all over the crime scene without realizing it.

## Summary / Key Takeaways

- **Overconfidence Leads to Failure:** Despite studying Shotover Grange for two weeks, Horace's plan failed due to unpredictable human factors.
- **Current Situation:** Horace is now serving time as an assistant librarian in prison, frequently bitter about the clever lady in red who outsmarted him—proving that **trust among thieves is an illusion**.
