# Sexual Division of Labour

A system in which all work inside the home is either done by the women of the family or organized by them through domestic helpers, while men work outside the home.

- **Public/Private Divide:** Domestic labor performed by women is unpaid and not valued, reducing their visibility in public and political life.
- **Feminism:** A movement or ideology advocating for equal rights, opportunities, and political power for women in society.
