## Meaning

Zollverein was a customs union formed by several German states to remove internal trade barriers and promote economic unity.

## Formation

- Established in **1834**.
- Led by **Prussia**.
- Most German states gradually joined the union.
- It created a unified market among member states.

## Main Features

- Removed customs duties between member states.
- Reduced the number of trade barriers.
- Encouraged the movement of goods between German states.
- Standardised systems of weights, measures and currencies.
- Promoted economic cooperation.

## Importance

- Zollverein helped strengthen economic unity among the German states.
- It encouraged trade and industrial development.
- It helped create a sense of economic nationalism.
- Prussia gained greater influence over the German states.
- It created favourable conditions for later political unification.

## Link with German Unification

Zollverein did not directly unite Germany politically.

However, it increased cooperation among German states and strengthened Prussia's position.

The economic unity created by Zollverein helped prepare the ground for the political unification of Germany in **1871**.
