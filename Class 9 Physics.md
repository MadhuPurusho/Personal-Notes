### 1. Motion

- **Speed ($v$):** $v = \frac{s}{t}$ _(where $s$ = distance, $t$ = time)_
    
- **Acceleration ($a$):** $a = \frac{v - u}{t}$ _(where $u$ = initial velocity, $v$ = final velocity)_
    
- **First Equation of Motion:** $v = u + at$
    
- **Second Equation of Motion:** $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
    
- **Third Equation of Motion:** $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
    

### 2. Force and Laws of Motion

- **Momentum ($p$):** $p = mv$ _(where $m$ = mass, $v$ = velocity)_
    
- **Force ($F$):** $F = ma$ _(where $m$ = mass, $a$ = acceleration)_
    
- **Conservation of Momentum:** $m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2$
    

### 3. Gravitation

- **Universal Law of Gravitation:** $F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}$ _(where $G$ is the gravitational constant)_
    
- **Acceleration due to Gravity ($g$):** $g = \frac{GM}{R^2}$ _(where $M$ = mass of Earth, $R$ = radius of Earth)_
    
- **Weight ($W$):** $W = mg$
    
- **Pressure ($P$):** $P = \frac{F}{A}$ _(where $F$ = force/thrust, $A$ = area)_
    
- **Relative Density:** $\text{Relative Density} = \frac{\text{Density of substance}}{\text{Density of water}}$
    

### 4. Work and Energy

- **Work ($W$):** $W = Fs$ _(where $F$ = force, $s$ = displacement)_
    
- **Kinetic Energy ($E_k$):** $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
    
- **Potential Energy ($E_p$):** $E_p = mgh$ _(where $h$ = height)_
    
- **Power ($P$):** $P = \frac{W}{t}$ _(where $W$ = work done, $t$ = time)_
    

### 5. Sound

- **Wave Velocity ($v$):** $v = \lambda \nu$ _(where $\lambda$ = wavelength, $\nu$ = frequency)_
    
- **Time Period ($T$):** $T = \frac{1}{\nu}$
