# Forms of Communalism in Politics

- **Everyday Beliefs:** Stereotypes, religious prejudices, and beliefs in the superiority of one's religion over others.
- **Majoritarian Dominance:** Quest for political dominance by the majority community, often alienating minority groups.
- **Political Mobilization:** Emotional appeals using religious symbols, leaders, and sacred texts to gather voters.
- **Communal Violence:** Extreme forms resulting in riots, massacres, and civil strife (e.g., India's partition violence).
