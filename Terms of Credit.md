Terms of credit are the conditions under which a lender provides a loan to a borrower.

## Main Terms

### 1. Interest Rate

- The interest rate is the extra amount charged by the lender on the loan.
- It is usually expressed as a percentage of the loan amount.
- A higher interest rate means the borrower pays more.

### 2. Collateral

- Collateral is an asset that the borrower offers as security against the loan.
- Examples:
  - Land
  - House
  - Vehicle
  - Livestock
- If the borrower fails to repay the loan, the lender may use the collateral to recover the money.

### 3. Documentation

- Borrowers may need to provide documents to obtain a loan.
- Examples:
  - Identity proof
  - Address proof
  - Income-related documents
  - Property documents

### 4. Mode of Repayment

The borrower and lender agree on how the loan will be repaid.

Examples:
- Monthly installments
- Quarterly payments
- One-time repayment

### 5. Duration of Loan

- This refers to the period within which the borrower must repay the loan.
- It may range from a few months to several years.

## Key Point

The terms of credit determine how much the borrower has to repay, how long they have to repay it, and what security or documents are required.
