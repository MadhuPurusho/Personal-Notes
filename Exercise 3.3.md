### **Question 1: Solve the following pair of linear equations by the elimination method and the substitution method.**

#### **(i) $x + y = 5$ and $2x - 3y = 4$**

**Elimination Method:**

1. Multiply Equation (1) by $3$:
    
    $$3x + 3y = 15 \quad \text{--- (Equation 3)}$$
    
2. Add Equation (2) and Equation (3):
    
    $$(2x - 3y) + (3x + 3y) = 4 + 15$$
    
    $$5x = 19 \implies \mathbf{x = \frac{19}{5}}$$
    
3. Substitute $x = \frac{19}{5}$ into Equation (1):
    
    $$\frac{19}{5} + y = 5 \implies y = 5 - \frac{19}{5} \implies \mathbf{y = \frac{6}{5}}$$
    

**Answer:** $x = \frac{19}{5}, \quad y = \frac{6}{5}$

#### **(ii) $3x + 4y = 10$ and $2x - 2y = 2$**

**Elimination Method:**

1. Multiply Equation (2) by $2$:
    
    $$4x - 4y = 4 \quad \text{--- (Equation 3)}$$
    
2. Add Equation (1) and Equation (3):
    
    $$(3x + 4y) + (4x - 4y) = 10 + 4$$
    
    $$7x = 14 \implies \mathbf{x = 2}$$
    
3. Substitute $x = 2$ into Equation (1):
    
    $$3(2) + 4y = 10 \implies 6 + 4y = 10 \implies 4y = 4 \implies \mathbf{y = 1}$$
    

**Answer:** $x = 2, \quad y = 1$

#### **(iii) $3x - 5y - 4 = 0$ and $9x = 2y + 7$**

Rearrange equations:

- $3x - 5y = 4 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
    
- $9x - 2y = 7 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
    

**Elimination Method:**

1. Multiply Equation (1) by $3$:
    
    $$9x - 15y = 12 \quad \text{--- (Equation 3)}$$
    
2. Subtract Equation (3) from Equation (2):
    
    $$(9x - 2y) - (9x - 15y) = 7 - 12$$
    
    $$13y = -5 \implies \mathbf{y = -\frac{5}{13}}$$
    
3. Substitute $y = -\frac{5}{13}$ into Equation (1):
    
    $$3x - 5\left(-\frac{5}{13}\right) = 4 \implies 3x + \frac{25}{13} = 4 \implies 3x = \frac{27}{13} \implies \mathbf{x = \frac{9}{13}}$$
    

**Answer:** $x = \frac{9}{13}, \quad y = -\frac{5}{13}$

#### **(iv) $\frac{x}{2} + \frac{2y}{3} = -1$ and $x - \frac{y}{3} = 3$**

Clear denominators by multiplying by LCM:

- $3x + 4y = -6 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
    
- $3x - y = 9 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
    

**Elimination Method:**

1. Subtract Equation (2) from Equation (1):
    
    $$(3x + 4y) - (3x - y) = -6 - 9$$
    
    $$5y = -15 \implies \mathbf{y = -3}$$
    
2. Substitute $y = -3$ into Equation (2):
    
    $$3x - (-3) = 9 \implies 3x + 3 = 9 \implies 3x = 6 \implies \mathbf{x = 2}$$
    

**Answer:** $x = 2, \quad y = -3$

### **Question 2: Form the pair of linear equations in the following problems, and find their solutions by the elimination method.**

#### **(i) If we add 1 to the numerator and subtract 1 from the denominator, a fraction reduces to 1. It becomes $\frac{1}{2}$ if we only add 1 to the denominator. What is the fraction?**

**Solution:**

Let the fraction be $\frac{x}{y}$.

- **Condition 1:** $\frac{x + 1}{y - 1} = 1 \implies x + 1 = y - 1 \implies x - y = -2 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
    
- **Condition 2:** $\frac{x}{y + 1} = \frac{1}{2} \implies 2x = y + 1 \implies 2x - y = 1 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
    

Subtract Equation (1) from Equation (2):

$$(2x - y) - (x - y) = 1 - (-2)$$

$$\mathbf{x = 3}$$

Substitute $x = 3$ into Equation (1):

$$3 - y = -2 \implies \mathbf{y = 5}$$

**Answer:** The fraction is **$\frac{3}{5}$**.

#### **(ii) Five years ago, Nuri was thrice as old as Sonu. Ten years later, Nuri will be twice as old as Sonu. How old are Nuri and Sonu?**

**Solution:**

Let Nuri's present age = $x$ years and Sonu's present age = $y$ years.

- **5 years ago:** $(x - 5) = 3(y - 5) \implies x - 3y = -10 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
    
- **10 years later:** $(x + 10) = 2(y + 10) \implies x - 2y = 10 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
    

Subtract Equation (1) from Equation (2):

$$(x - 2y) - (x - 3y) = 10 - (-10)$$

$$\mathbf{y = 20}$$

Substitute $y = 20$ into Equation (1):

$$x - 3(20) = -10 \implies x - 60 = -10 \implies \mathbf{x = 50}$$

**Answer:** Nuri's age = **$50$ years**, Sonu's age = **$20$ years**.

#### **(iii) The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the number.**

**Solution:**

Let the tens digit be $y$ and unit digit be $x$.

- Original number = $10y + x$
    
- Reversed number = $10x + y$
    

1. **Sum of digits:**
    
    $$x + y = 9 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$$
    
2. **Given condition:**
    
    $$9(10y + x) = 2(10x + y)$$
    
    $$90y + 9x = 20x + 2y$$
    
    $$88y = 11x \implies \mathbf{x - 8y = 0} \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$$
    

Subtract Equation (2) from Equation (1):

$$(x + y) - (x - 8y) = 9 - 0$$

$$9y = 9 \implies \mathbf{y = 1}$$

Substitute $y = 1$ into Equation (1):

$$x + 1 = 9 \implies \mathbf{x = 8}$$

Original number = $10(1) + 8 = 18$.

**Answer:** The number is **$18$**.

#### **(iv) Meena went to a bank to withdraw ₹2000. She asked the cashier to give her ₹50 and ₹100 notes only. Meena got 25 notes in all. Find how many notes of ₹50 and ₹100 she received.**

**Solution:**

Let number of ₹50 notes = $x$ and number of ₹100 notes = $y$.

- Total notes = $25 \implies x + y = 25 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
    
- Total value = ₹2000 $\implies 50x + 100y = 2000 \implies x + 2y = 40 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
    

Subtract Equation (1) from Equation (2):

$$(x + 2y) - (x + y) = 40 - 25$$

$$\mathbf{y = 15}$$

Substitute $y = 15$ into Equation (1):

$$x + 15 = 25 \implies \mathbf{x = 10}$$

**Answer:** **$10$ notes of ₹50** and **$15$ notes of ₹100**.

#### **(v) A lending library has a fixed charge for the first three days and an additional charge for each day thereafter. Saritha paid ₹27 for a book kept for seven days, while Susy paid ₹21 for the book she kept for five days. Find the fixed charge and the charge for each extra day.**

**Solution:**

Let fixed charge (for first 3 days) = ₹$x$ and charge per extra day = ₹$y$.

- **Saritha (7 days = 3 fixed + 4 extra):**
    
    $$x + 4y = 27 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$$
    
- **Susy (5 days = 3 fixed + 2 extra):**
    
    $$x + 2y = 21 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$$
    

Subtract Equation (2) from Equation (1):

$$(x + 4y) - (x + 2y) = 27 - 21$$

$$2y = 6 \implies \mathbf{y = 3}$$

Substitute $y = 3$ into Equation (2):

$$x + 2(3) = 21 \implies x + 6 = 21 \implies \mathbf{x = 15}$$

**Answer:** Fixed charge = **₹15**, Charge per extra day = **₹3**.
