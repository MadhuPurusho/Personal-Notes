Power is divided among different organs of government placed at the same level—**Legislature, Executive, and Judiciary**.

```
                 ┌────────────────────────────────┐
                 │ HORIZONTAL POWER DISTRIBUTION  │
                 └───────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
         ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
         ▼                       ▼                       ▼
   ┌───────────┐           ┌───────────┐           ┌───────────┐
   │ Legislature│          │ Executive │           │ Judiciary │
   └───────────┘           └───────────┘           └───────────┘
```

- **System of Checks and Balances:** None of the organs can exercise unlimited power. Each organ checks the others (e.g., judges are appointed by the executive but can review executive actions and laws passed by the legislature).
