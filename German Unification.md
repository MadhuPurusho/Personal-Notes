# Unification of Germany

## Background

Before 1871, Germany was not a single unified country.

- The German-speaking region was divided into many states.
- The most powerful states were Austria and Prussia.
- Prussia gradually became the main force behind German unification.
- Nationalist ideas encouraged people to demand a united Germany.

## Role of Prussia

Prussia took the lead in the movement for German unification.

Its important advantages were:

- Strong army
- Powerful bureaucracy
- Growing industries
- Economic influence through the Zollverein

## Otto von Bismarck

Otto von Bismarck was the Chief Minister of Prussia.

He became the main political leader of German unification.

Bismarck believed that German unification would be achieved through military strength and practical politics.

His policy is commonly associated with the phrase "Blood and Iron".

## Wars of Unification

Bismarck used three wars to achieve German unification.

### 1. War with Denmark, 1864

- Prussia and Austria fought Denmark.
- Denmark was defeated.
- Prussia and Austria gained control of Schleswig and Holstein.

### 2. Austro-Prussian War, 1866

- Prussia fought Austria.
- Prussia defeated Austria.
- Austria was excluded from the process of German unification.
- Prussia became the dominant power among the German states.

### 3. Franco-Prussian War, 1870–1871

- Prussia and the German states fought France.
- France was defeated.
- The victory created strong nationalist feelings among the German states.
- The southern German states joined the Prussian-led unification process.

## Formation of the German Empire

In January 1871, the German states were united into a German Empire.

- The German Empire was proclaimed at the Palace of Versailles.
- The Prussian King, Wilhelm I, was declared German Emperor.
- Bismarck became the first Chancellor of the German Empire.

## Importance of German Unification

- Germany became a unified nation-state.
- Prussia became the dominant German state.
- The German Empire became a major European power.
- Political and economic unity strengthened Germany.
