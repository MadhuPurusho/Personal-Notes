## 🗺️ Map Location Guide

- **Salal Project**: Northernmost dam on the map, located in Jammu & Kashmir on the Chenab River[cite: 1].
- **Bhakra Nangal**: Located near the Himachal Pradesh-Punjab border on the Satluj River[cite: 1].
- **Tehri**: Located in Uttarakhand on the Bhagirathi River (tributary of Ganga)[cite: 1].
- **Rana Pratap Sagar**: Located in south-eastern Rajasthan on the Chambal River[cite: 1].
- **Sardar Sarovar**: Located in western Gujarat near the Narmada River outlet[cite: 1].
- **Hirakud**: Located in eastern Odisha along the Mahanadi River[cite: 1].
- **Nagarjuna Sagar**: Located on the border between Telangana and Andhra Pradesh along the Krishna River[cite: 1].
- **Tungabhadra**: Located in central Karnataka along the Tungabhadra River[cite: 1].
