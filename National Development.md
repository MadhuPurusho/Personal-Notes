## Meaning

National development refers to the development and progress of a country as a whole.

It considers the well-being of all people, not just the development of a particular group or region.

## Main Goals

- Increase people's income
- Improve education
- Provide better healthcare
- Create employment opportunities
- Reduce poverty and inequality
- Provide basic facilities
- Ensure equality and freedom
- Protect the environment
- Improve infrastructure
- Maintain peace and security

## Important Points

- Different people may have different ideas about national development.
- National development should benefit different sections of society.
- The development of a country should consider both economic and non-economic goals.
- A country's development cannot be judged only by its income.
