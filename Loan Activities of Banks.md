## Meaning

Banks accept deposits from people and use a major portion of these deposits to provide loans to borrowers.

This is one of the main activities of banks.

## How Banks Provide Loans

1. People deposit their money in banks.
2. Banks keep a small portion of the deposits as cash to meet daily withdrawal needs.
3. Banks use the remaining deposits to provide loans.
4. Borrowers repay the loans with interest.
5. The interest earned on loans is an important source of income for banks.

## Example

Suppose people deposit ₹10,000 in a bank.

- The bank keeps some money as cash reserves.
- It lends the remaining amount to borrowers.
- Borrowers repay the loan with interest.

The bank pays interest to depositors and charges a higher interest rate from borrowers.

## Importance of Bank Loans

Bank loans help people:

- Start businesses.
- Buy agricultural inputs.
- Expand existing businesses.
- Purchase houses and other assets.
- Meet various financial needs.

## Key Point

Banks act as intermediaries between people who have surplus money and people who need money.

### Money Flow

People → Deposits → Banks → Loans → Borrowers

Borrowers → Repayment + Interest → Banks
