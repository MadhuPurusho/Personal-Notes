# What is Federalism

**Federalism** is a system of government in which power is divided between a central authority and various constituent units of the country.

- **Federal Government:** Power divided between two or more tiers, each with its own jurisdiction protected by constitution, e.g. India, Belgium, USA.
- **Unitary Government:** Power concentrated in central government, sub-units are subordinate, e.g. Sri Lanka, China.
