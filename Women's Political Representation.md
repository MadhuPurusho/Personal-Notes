# Women's Political Representation

- **Central & State Legislatures:** Low proportion of women in politics. Historically, women's representation in the Lok Sabha crossed 14% for the first time in 2019, while state assemblies remain below 5%.
- **Local Self-Government:** The 73rd and 74th Amendments reserved one-third (33%) of seats in Panchayats and Municipalities for women, bringing over 10 lakh elected women representatives into local administration.
- **Women's Reservation Act (2023):** Mandates 33% reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.
