## Who was Giuseppe Mazzini?

Giuseppe Mazzini was an Italian revolutionary and nationalist who played an important role in the movement for the unification of Italy.

## Important Facts

- Born in **1805** in Genoa.
- He became a member of the secret society **Carbonari**.
- Founded **Young Italy** in **1831**.
- Founded **Young Europe** in **1834**.
- Believed Italy should become a **united, independent republic**.
- Supported the idea of a united Europe based on democratic principles.

## Role in Italian Unification

- Mazzini spread the ideas of nationalism and republicanism.
- He wanted to unite the Italian states into one nation.
- He inspired young people to participate in nationalist movements.
- His ideas influenced later leaders and movements involved in Italian unification.
