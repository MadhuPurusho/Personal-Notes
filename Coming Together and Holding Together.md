# Coming Together and Holding Together

Two routes to form a federation:

- **Coming Together Federations:**
    - Independent states come together to form a union.
    - Units are strong, centre is weak by comparison.
    - All units have equal power.
    - e.g. USA, Switzerland, Australia.

- **Holding Together Federations:**
    - Large country divides power between centre and states.
    - Centre is stronger than states.
    - Some units may have unequal / special powers.
    - e.g. India, Belgium, Spain.
