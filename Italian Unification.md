## Background

Before unification, Italy was divided into several states.

- Northern Italy was under Austrian control or influence.
- The centre included the Papal States.
- The south was ruled by the Bourbon monarchy.
- The Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont was ruled by the Italian royal family.
- Nationalists wanted to unite these states into one Italian nation.

## Giuseppe Mazzini

Giuseppe Mazzini was an important Italian revolutionary and nationalist.

- Founded Young Italy in 1831.
- Wanted Italy to become a united and independent republic.
- Spread nationalist ideas among young Italians.
- His revolutionary attempts were unsuccessful, but his ideas inspired the movement for unification.

## Count Camillo de Cavour

Cavour was the Chief Minister of Sardinia-Piedmont.

- He played the leading role in the political unification of Italy.
- He used diplomacy and alliances to strengthen Sardinia-Piedmont.
- He formed an alliance with France against Austria.
- Sardinia-Piedmont defeated Austria in 1859.
- Several northern and central Italian states later joined Sardinia-Piedmont.

## Giuseppe Garibaldi

Garibaldi was a famous Italian revolutionary and military leader.

- In 1860, he led a force of volunteers known as the Red Shirts.
- He invaded the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies in southern Italy.
- He defeated the Bourbon rulers.
- He handed the conquered territories to Victor Emmanuel II.

## Victor Emmanuel II

Victor Emmanuel II was the King of Sardinia-Piedmont.

- He became the first king of unified Italy.
- He played an important role in completing political unification.

## Main Stages of Unification

### 1859

- Sardinia-Piedmont, with French support, fought Austria.
- Austria was defeated.
- Lombardy became part of Sardinia-Piedmont.

### 1860

- Garibaldi and his Red Shirts conquered Sicily and Naples.
- Garibaldi handed these territories to Victor Emmanuel II.

### 1861

- The Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed.
- Victor Emmanuel II became its first king.
- However, Venetia and Rome were still outside the new kingdom.

### 1866

- Venetia became part of Italy after the Austro-Prussian War.

### 1870

- Italian forces captured Rome.
- Rome became the capital of Italy.

## Role of Different Leaders

| Leader | Main Role |
|---|---|
| Giuseppe Mazzini | Spread nationalist and republican ideas |
| Cavour | Used diplomacy and war to expand Sardinia-Piedmont |
| Giuseppe Garibaldi | Led military campaigns in southern Italy |
| Victor Emmanuel II | Became the first king of unified Italy |

## Important Difference

Mazzini → Ideas and nationalism

Cavour → Diplomacy and political leadership

Garibaldi → Military campaigns

Victor Emmanuel II → Monarchy and political unification
