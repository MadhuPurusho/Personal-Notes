# Status of Women in Indian Society

India remains a patriarchal society (male-dominated), where women face systematic disadvantage:

- **Literacy Rate:** Significantly lower among women (~54%) compared to men (~76%).
- **High-Paid Jobs:** Small percentage of women occupy highly paid or senior administrative positions.
- **Wage Inequality:** Despite the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976, women across sports, cinema, and agriculture are frequently paid less than men for equal work.
- **Sex-Ratio and Preference for Sons:** Gender-biased sex selection and female foeticide led to a decline in child sex ratio across several regions.
