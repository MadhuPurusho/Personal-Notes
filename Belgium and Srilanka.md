# Belgium
Belgium is a small European country with borders shared with France, the Netherlands, Germany, and Luxembourg.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   BELGIUM POPULATION                     │
├───────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ Flemish Region (59%)      │ Wallonia Region (40%)        │
│ Speaks: Dutch             │ Speaks: French               │
└───────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
                    1% German-speaking
```

- **Overall Country:** 59% Dutch-speaking (Flemish region), 40% French-speaking (Wallonia region), 1% German-speaking.
    
- **Capital City (Brussels):** 80% French-speaking, 20% Dutch-speaking.

The minority French-speaking population was relatively wealthy and influential. The majority Dutch-speaking population received benefits of economic development and education much later, leading to tension in the 1950s and 1960s.

# Sri lanka
Sri Lanka is an island nation located off the southern coast of Tamil Nadu, India, which gained independence in 1948.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  SRI LANKA POPULATION                    │
├───────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ Sinhala Speakers (74%)    │ Tamil Speakers (18%)         │
│ Religion: Buddhist        │ Sri Lankan Tamils (13%)      │
│                           │ Indian Tamils (5%)           │
└───────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
            7% Christians (both Sinhala & Tamil)
```

- **Sinhala Speakers:** 74% of the population (mostly Buddhist).
    
- **Tamil Speakers:** 18% of the population (Hindus or Muslims).
    
    - **Sri Lankan Tamils (13%):** Native Tamils of the island.
        
    - **Indian Tamils (5%):** Descendants of plantation workers brought from India during colonial rule.
        
- **Christians:** 7% (includes both Sinhala and Tamil speakers).
