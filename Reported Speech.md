# Class 10 English Grammar | Board Exam Notes

## Chapter: Reported Speech (Direct & Indirect Speech)
*CBSE English Language and Literature (Grammar Section)*

> **Core Concept:** **Direct Speech** quotes the exact words spoken by a person enclosed in quotation marks. **Indirect (Reported) Speech** conveys the essence of what was said without using the exact words or quotation marks.

---

## Key Categories of Changes

| Category | Primary Rule | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Punctuation** | Remove quotation marks and commas separating the reporting clause. Add connecting conjunctions (*that, if, whether*). | *"I am fine," he said.* $\rightarrow$ *He said that he was fine.* |
| **Pronouns** | Shift according to the **S-O-N Rule** (1st Person $\rightarrow$ Subject, 2nd Person $\rightarrow$ Object, 3rd Person $\rightarrow$ No Change). | *She said to me, "You helped me."* $\rightarrow$ *She told me that I had helped her.* |
| **Time & Place** | Shift words denoting nearness in time/place to words denoting distance. | *now* $\rightarrow$ *then*, *today* $\rightarrow$ *that day*. |
| **Tenses** | Shift tenses one step back into the past if the reporting verb is in the past tense. | *Simple Present* $\rightarrow$ *Simple Past*. |

---

## Comprehensive Rules & Shift Tables

### 1. Change in Tenses (Backshifting)

> **Golden Rule:** Backshift tenses ONLY if the reporting verb (e.g., *said*, *told*) is in the **past tense**. If the reporting verb is in the **present** (*says*) or **future** (*will say*), the tense inside reported speech **does not change**.

| Direct Speech Tense | Reported Speech Tense | Direct Speech Example | Reported Speech Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Simple Present** | **Simple Past** | *"I play cricket," he said.* | *He said that he played cricket.* |
| **Present Continuous** | **Past Continuous** | *"She is reading," said Tom.* | *Tom said that she was reading.* |
| **Present Perfect** | **Past Perfect** | *"I have done it," he said.* | *He said that he had done it.* |
| **Present Perfect Cont.** | **Past Perfect Cont.** | *"I have been waiting," she said.* | *She said that she had been waiting.* |
| **Simple Past** | **Past Perfect** | *"I bought a car," he said.* | *He said that he had bought a car.* |
| **Past Continuous** | **Past Perfect Cont.** | *"We were playing," they said.* | *They said that they had been playing.* |
| **Past Perfect / Cont.** | **NO CHANGE** | *"I had left," she said.* | *She said that she had left.* |

#### Exceptions to Tense Change (Must-Know for Boards):
1. **Universal Truths / Scientific Facts:** Tense remains unchanged.
   - *Direct:* The teacher said, "The Earth revolves around the Sun."
   - *Reported:* The teacher said that the Earth revolves around the Sun.
2. **Habitual Actions:** Tense remains unchanged.
   - *Direct:* He said, "I wake up at 5 AM every day."
   - *Reported:* He said that he wakes up at 5 AM every day.

---

### 2. Changes in Modals, Time & Place Adverbials

#### A. Modal Verbs
- **will / shall** $\rightarrow$ **would / should**
- **can** $\rightarrow$ **could**
- **may** $\rightarrow$ **might**
- **must** $\rightarrow$ **had to** (or remains **must** for moral duties/laws)
- **could / would / should / might / ought to** $\rightarrow$ **NO CHANGE**

#### B. Words Denoting Time and Distance

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
| :--- | :--- |
| **now** | then |
| **today** | that day |
| **yesterday** | the previous day / the day before |
| **tomorrow** | the next day / the following day |
| **this / these** | that / those |
| **here** | there |
| **ago** | before |
| **last night / week** | the previous night / week |
| **next week / month** | the following week / month |

## Sentence-Wise Conversion Rules

### 1. Assertive Sentences (Statements)
- **Reporting Verb:** Change *said to* $\rightarrow$ *told*. Keep *said* as *said* if there is no object.
- **Conjunction:** Use **that**.
- *Example:* She said to me, "I am going to London tomorrow."
  $\rightarrow$ She **told** me **that** she was going to London the **next day**.

### 2. Interrogative Sentences (Questions)
- **Reporting Verb:** Change *said / said to* $\rightarrow$ *asked / inquired / demanded*.
- **Sentence Structure:** Convert the question structure into assertive structure ($S + V$). Remove the question mark.
- **Yes/No Questions:** Connect using **if** or **whether**.
  - *Example:* He said to me, "Are you coming?"
    $\rightarrow$ He **asked** me **if** I was coming.
- **Wh- Questions:** Retain the Wh- word (*where, what, why, how, who*) as the connector.
  - *Example:* The teacher said, "Where do you live?"
    $\rightarrow$ The teacher **asked** **where** I lived.

### 3. Imperative Sentences (Commands, Requests, Advice)
- **Reporting Verb:** Change to *ordered, requested, advised, instructed, suggested, forbade* according to tone.
- **Structure:** Replace quotation marks with **to + base verb ($V_1$)**. For negative imperatives, use **not to + $V_1$**.
  - *Example 1:* Anish said to Sid, "Please lend me the book."
    $\rightarrow$ Anish **requested** Sid **to lend** him the book.
  - *Example 2:* The doctor said to him, "Do not smoke."
    $\rightarrow$ The doctor **advised** him **not to** smoke.

### 4. Exclamatory Sentences
- **Reporting Verb:** Change to *exclaimed with joy, exclaimed with sorrow, exclaimed with surprise, cried out*.
- **Conjunction:** Use **that**.
- *Example:* Shivina said, "Alas! I am lost."
  $\rightarrow$ Shivina **exclaimed sadly that** she was lost.

## Important Practice Questions & Model Answers

### Type 1: Dialogue Completion (CBSE Board Pattern)

**Dialogue:**
> **Father:** Where are you going now?
> **Son:** I am going to the market to buy some vegetables.

**Incomplete Report:**
> The father asked his son (a) _______________. The son replied (b) _______________.

> **Answers:**
> - **(a)** where he was going then
> - **(b)** that he was going to the market to buy some vegetables

---

### Type 2: Direct Error Correction / Editing

1. **Incorrect:** *She asked me that where I was staying.*
   - **Correct:** *She asked me **where** I was staying.* *(Reason: Do not use 'that' before Wh- question words).*
2. **Incorrect:** *He ordered me to not touch the wires.*
   - **Correct:** *He ordered me **not to** touch the wires.* *(Reason: Imperative negative uses 'not to', not 'to not').*
3. **Incorrect:** *The teacher asked if I have completed my work.*
   - **Correct:** *The teacher asked if I **had** completed my work.* *(Reason: Past reporting verb requires backshifting).*

---

## Summary / Key Takeaways

- **Question Word Order:** In reported questions, place the subject BEFORE the verb (*where I was going*, NOT *where was I going*).
- **Double Conjunction Error:** Never combine *that* with *if*, *whether*, or *Wh- words*.
- **Reporting Verb Selection:** Match the reporting verb with the emotion/intent (*requested* for please, *ordered* for commands).
