# How Federalism is Practised

**1. Linguistic States:**
- States created on basis of language to ensure same language speakers in same state.
- First: Andhra Pradesh (Telugu) in 1953. Later: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana, Kerala etc.
- 2014: Telangana carved out of Andhra Pradesh.
- Result: Made administration easier, strengthened unity.

**2. Language Policy:**
- No National Language. Hindi (Devanagari script) is Official Language.
- 22 languages in 8th Schedule. English also official.
- States have their own official languages.
- Careful policy avoided strife unlike Sri Lanka.

**3. Centre-State Relations:**
- Before 1990: Same party at Centre and most states -> Centre dominated, misused Article 356 to dismiss state govts.
- After 1990: Rise of regional parties, coalition governments -> Real power-sharing, Centre respects autonomy.
- Supreme Court restricted arbitrary dismissal of state govts.

> **Key Outcome:** Federalism strengthened unity while accommodating diversity.
