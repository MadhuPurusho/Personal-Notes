**Federalism** is a system of government in which power is divided between a central authority and various constituent units of the country

### Key Features of Federalism

1. **Two or More Levels:** Government operates with two or more tiers (e.g., Union, State, Local).
    
2. **Jurisdiction:** Each tier has its own jurisdiction in specific matters of legislation, taxation, and administration, guaranteed by the constitution.
    
3. **Constitutional Protection:** The existence and authority of each tier is constitutionally guaranteed.
    
4. **Rigid Constitution:** Fundamental provisions of the constitution cannot be unilaterally changed by one level of government; consent of both levels is required.
    
5. **Role of Judiciary:** Courts interpret the constitution. The highest court acts as an umpire if disputes arise between different levels of government.
    
6. **Financial Autonomy:** Sources of revenue for each level are clearly specified to ensure financial independence.
    
7. **Dual Objectives:** To safeguard and promote unity of the country, while accommodating regional diversity.
