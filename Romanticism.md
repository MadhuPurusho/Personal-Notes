## Meaning

Romanticism was a cultural movement that developed in Europe during the late 18th and 19th centuries.

It focused on emotions, imagination, intuition, and a sense of shared cultural identity rather than only reason and rationality.

## Romanticism and Nationalism

Romantic artists and thinkers helped develop nationalist feelings by:

- Glorifying folk traditions and culture.
- Promoting local languages.
- Collecting folk songs, poetry and stories.
- Celebrating national history and traditions.
- Creating a sense of shared heritage.

## Role in Germany

In Germany, thinkers such as the Grimm Brothers collected and published traditional German folk tales.

They believed that folk culture helped create a sense of national identity.

## Importance

- Helped spread nationalist ideas.
- Preserved folk traditions and languages.
- Created a shared cultural identity.
- Connected nationalism with art, literature and music.
