## Who was Duke Metternich?

Duke Klemens von Metternich was the Austrian Chancellor and a leading conservative statesman in Europe during the 19th century.

## Role in European Politics

- He opposed liberalism and nationalism.
- He wanted to preserve the traditional political order in Europe.
- He played an important role in the Congress of Vienna in 1815.
- He supported restoring monarchies that had been overthrown during the Napoleonic period.

## Metternich and Nationalism

Metternich considered nationalism and liberalism threats to the existing monarchies.

He worked to suppress nationalist movements in Europe, including movements in the German and Italian states.

## Metternich System

The conservative governments supported censorship and restrictions on political activities.

In the German states, the Carlsbad Decrees of 1819:

- Censored newspapers.
- Restricted liberal political activities.
- Increased government control over universities.
- Suppressed nationalist and liberal organisations.

## Importance

Metternich became one of the main symbols of conservative politics in post-Napoleonic Europe.
