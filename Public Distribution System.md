## Meaning

The Public Distribution System (PDS) is a government system that distributes essential food items to people at subsidised prices through fair price shops.

## Main Objectives

- Provide food security to people.
- Make essential food items available at affordable prices.
- Protect poor and vulnerable households from hunger.
- Control fluctuations in food prices.
- Maintain a buffer stock of food grains.
- Support farmers by purchasing food grains at minimum support prices.

## How PDS Works

1. The government purchases food grains from farmers.
2. The Food Corporation of India (FCI) stores the food grains.
3. The government supplies these food grains to fair price shops.
4. Eligible households purchase food grains and other essential items at subsidised prices.

## Fair Price Shops

Fair price shops are also known as ration shops.

They distribute essential commodities to eligible consumers at prices fixed by the government.

Commonly distributed items include:

- Rice
- Wheat
- Sugar
- Kerosene

## Role of PDS in Food Security

PDS helps ensure that people can access food even when:

- Food prices increase.
- Production falls.
- Natural disasters affect food supply.
- Poor households cannot afford food at market prices.

## Types of Ration Cards

### Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)

- Meant for the poorest households.
- Provides food grains at highly subsidised prices.

### Priority Households (PHH)

- Covers households identified as eligible under the National Food Security Act.
- Provides subsidised food grains.

## Importance of PDS

- Reduces hunger and food insecurity.
- Provides food at affordable prices.
- Helps poor families.
- Stabilises food prices.
- Helps maintain food grain stocks.
- Supports the government's food security programmes.

## Problems of PDS

- Leakage and diversion of food grains.
- Corruption.
- Poor quality of food grains in some cases.
- Irregular functioning of some ration shops.
- Some deserving families may be excluded.
- Food grains may not always reach the intended beneficiaries.

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PDS | System for distributing essential commodities at subsidised prices |
| FCI | Food Corporation of India, responsible for procurement and storage of food grains |
| Fair Price Shop | Government-authorised shop that sells subsidised food grains |
| Subsidy | Government financial support that reduces the price paid by consumers |
| Buffer Stock | Stock of food grains maintained by the government for food security |
| AAY | Scheme for the poorest households |
| PHH | Priority Households eligible for subsidised food grains |

## Exam Answer

### What is the Public Distribution System?

The Public Distribution System is a government system that distributes essential food items such as rice and wheat to eligible households through fair price shops at subsidised prices. It aims to provide food security, especially to poor and vulnerable sections of society.
