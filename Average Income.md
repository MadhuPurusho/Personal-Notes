# Average Income / Per Capita Income

## Meaning

Average income, also called per capita income, is the average income earned per person in a country.

## Formula

Per Capita Income = Total Income of the Country ÷ Total Population

## Example

If a country has:
- Total income = ₹10,00,000
- Population = 1,000 people

Per Capita Income = ₹10,00,000 ÷ 1,000 = ₹1,000

## Importance

- It is used to compare the average income of different countries.
- The World Bank uses per capita income as one of the criteria for classifying countries.
- Higher per capita income generally indicates a higher average income.

## Key Point

Per capita income shows the average income of people, but it does not show how income is distributed among them.

# Categorisation of Countries by Income

The World Bank categorises countries based on their per capita income.

## 1. High-Income Countries

- Countries with high per capita income.
- People generally have higher average incomes.
- Example: United States, Germany, Japan.

## 2. Middle-Income Countries

- Countries with per capita income between low-income and high-income levels.
- Example: India, Brazil, South Africa.

## 3. Low-Income Countries

- Countries with low per capita income.
- Average income per person is relatively low.
- Example: Afghanistan, Burundi.

## Key Point

Per capita income is used by the World Bank to compare and classify countries according to their average income.
