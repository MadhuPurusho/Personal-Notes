# Relation Between Religion and Politics

Religion enters politics through various frameworks:

- **Gandhian View:** Mahatma Gandhi believed politics cannot be separated from religion; he meant moral and ethical values drawn from all religions, not dogma.
- **Human Rights View:** Human rights groups argue that religious minorities are often victims of discrimination and demand special state protection.
- **Women's Movement View:** Demands reform of Family Laws (laws governing marriage, divorce, and adoption) across all religions to stop discrimination against women.
